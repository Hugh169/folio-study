# Changelog

## 1.0 (12 October 2026)

The first public release.

### Sorting
- Drop files on Folio, choose them with Add Files (⌘O), or send them from
  Shortcuts. Folio can also watch Downloads and other folders you choose, and
  sorts what arrives there.
- Reads documents, PDFs, scans and photos (text recognition), and recordings
  and videos (transcribed on your Mac).
- Files each one by subject, document type, year and exam, into real folders
  in your School folder. Every move can be undone, and nothing is ever deleted.
- Anything Folio isn't sure about waits under Needs a look. It learns from the
  subjects you choose.

### Assessments and planning
- Assessments and exams, with weighting, status, marks, feedback, milestones
  and linked files. Folio finds them in task sheets and asks before adding them.
- Plan My Week works back from what's due, around school, a Week A/B
  timetable and your Apple Calendar events (read only). Sessions you make or
  change are kept when Folio plans again.
- Each study session has a plan, and steps you don't finish move to the next
  one. Each subject has a to-do list.
- Optionally, assessments go in a Folio list in Apple Reminders.

### Studying
- A study timer in a small window and the menu bar, which records how it went.
- Flashcards written from your own notes, reviewed with spaced repetition.
- Search inside your files, "What's This?" summaries and related files.
- Past papers paired with their marking guidelines, with timed practice and
  marks.

### Progress
- Marks weighted by what each assessment is worth, study time against your
  plan and the work due, a weekly review, busy-week warnings and term
  summaries.

### Also
- Curriculum packs for NSW HSC, VCE, QCE, WACE, SACE, GCSE and A level, the IB
  Diploma, US high school and AP, Cambridge International, and university.
  Packs can be shared as `.foliopack` files.
- Back up and restore (`.foliobackup`).
- Claude as an optional helper, with your own API key, off by default.
- English, plus French, Spanish, German and Chinese (Simplified) in beta.

### Good to know
- Folio needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 27. The on-device AI needs
  Apple Intelligence; without it Folio still sorts files.
- Folio isn't notarized: open it the first time from System Settings › Privacy
  & Security › Open Anyway.
- The translations haven't been checked by native speakers yet.
